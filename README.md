TELUK SENJA is an original 2D top-down open-world crime game: explore a dusk-lit grid city on foot and by car, steal cars from the kerb, weave through traffic and pedestrians, outrun police cars as your wanted level rises, and finish three chained story missions as Raka, a night courier — pick up and deliver a package, escape a police raid to the safehouse, then settle the last debt at the old fish warehouse. It has combat on foot, a HUD with cash, health, wanted stars, objectives and a live minimap, title / pause / game-over / victory screens, best results saved in the browser, and fully synthesised WebAudio sound (engine hum, sirens, pickups) with no audio files and no network requests. Everything — city, characters, vehicles, story, art and sound — is original.

## Run it

node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080 — WASD move/drive, Enter steal/exit car, click shoot, Space punch, Shift run, P pause, M mute
